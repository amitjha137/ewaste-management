About e-waste management

Electronics are the fastest-growing waste stream on Earth. Here’s why recycling them properly matters, and how to do it.

01

What is e-waste?

E-waste refers to discarded electronic devices. Proper recycling prevents pollution and recovers valuable materials such as copper, gold and rare earths.

02

Why it matters

Phones, laptops and batteries leak toxic metals in landfills. Recovering them saves energy, water and carbon compared with mining new material.

How to recycle

  1. Deposit e-waste in designated binsFind a smart bin near you and check it accepts your item category.
  2. Participate in collection drivesJoin community events to hand over bulkier devices.
  3. Spread awarenessTell friends and family where to take their old electronics.
